athletIQ

A recruiting platform college track coaches use to find, compare and track recruits.

2023NOW

athletiq.run

  • Stanford, UNCamong programs under contract
  • $8Kbacking from Microsoft and AWS
  • 314commits since the 2025 rebuild
Detail AHow it works

One scale for every event

Every race converts to VDOT, Jack Daniels' oxygen-cost model, so an 800m runner and a miler can be compared.

VDOT converterThe same formulas the product uses.

65.5VDOT

Forecasts from training

A Banister fitness-fatigue model fit to each athlete, with 4,000 sampled parameter sets for an honest range.

Fitness-fatigue modelDrag the weekly mileage bars.

Benchmark before you ship

Three injury-risk models on 42,798 athlete-weeks, split by athlete. The simplest one won.

Pan-Out, for other sports

An athletIQ product that prices junior tennis, chess, baseball and hockey prospects with stacked gradient-boosted models. It beat ratings on simulated data (AUC 0.94 vs 0.84); on real ATP careers the edge nearly vanished (0.844 vs 0.841).

SECRET

My school's 48-volt electric car for the one-hour Electric Vehicle Grand Prix. I founded the team and lead the build.

2023NOW
  • 3DC Grand Prix raced
  • Top prizefor 3D printing
  • Dubaiinvited, 2027
Detail BHow it works

Most distance in one hour on one battery wins. We rebuilt the kit car with an aluminum chassis, a new battery pack, 3D-printed wheel covers and an Arduino pacing system. We raised $7K and work alongside UMD's Formula SAE team.

Pacing to an energy budget

Each lap, the Arduino spreads the remaining battery over the laps left and tells the driver to speed up or back off.

One-hour race simulatorA driver's speed, with and without pacing.

Modeled car with assumed values, not measured from ours.

Meridian

A model that ranks which investors fit a startup, built in UVA research with Prof. Adam Tashman to help underserved founders.

2025NOW

Featured in Technical.ly

  • 907Kstartup-investor pairs
  • 48,046investors ranked
  • 20,000+founders served
Detail CHow it works

A LightGBM LambdaRank model scores 16 features for every startup-investor pair, including text similarity between the startup and the investor's past portfolio. The split is by startup so no company leaks into testing. Presented at the UVA research symposium and ICMBF 2026; paper in review.

    Verifying papers on TDX

    Re-ran published business and economics papers inside Intel TDX confidential VMs, with Prof. Jiasun Li.

    20252025

    Read the abstract

    • 19 of 27replications succeeded
    • $1.33per paper
    • 2clouds tested
    Detail GHow it works

    Journals require replication packages, but nothing proves the code ran as claimed. TDX VMs isolate memory from the host and can attest to exactly what ran. We ran each paper on Azure and Google Cloud; most failures came from code missing from the package.
